# Lilin fleet review, 2026-11-17

Eight agents audited every screen of the web UI, owner tabs, share pages and TV apps (live screenshots at phone,
tablet, desktop and TV sizes, English + Swedish, plus a throwaway owner server), and researched competitors,
small-group social features and current media design. A three-person panel (product lead, design director,
skeptic engineer) argued it out over two rounds. Full reports: `fleet-2026-11-17/` (r1 = audits + research,
r2 = panel positions, r3 = rebuttals + final votes).

## The verdict
Lilin doesn't need more features first; it needs a finishing pass. 82 whats-new entries in 7 days produced drift:
42 font sizes, 44 radii, 93 shadows, 11 secondary-button heights, 4 bottom-sheet implementations, 5 focus styles.
The engineer verified 40 headline findings against the code: 33 real, 6 partial, 1 wrong.

## Fix now (panel consensus, in order)
1. **Privacy:** Restricted/quiet viewers were sent to every viewer in Live TV / Watch Together state and shown in
   the live chat header. Filter server-side.
2. **Player correctness:** Audio button hidden in rooms (leftover line), menus stacking, error box with no way out.
3. **Static caching + compression:** no ETag, no gzip/brotli; every cold open re-downloads ~1.5 MB (app.js 626 KB).
4. **Contrast:** primary buttons on Ember/Synthwave at 2.6-2.8:1; status greens invisible on Paper/Daylight.
5. **Family-facing wording:** offline screen titled "Trending this week" telling family to run server.js, raw
   "Failed to fetch", English-only invite/watch pages, unreadable SVT title sheet.
6. **Screenshot harness starts real encodes** (GET `/api/media/.../audio` queues H.264 copies). Stub it; later move
   queueing to a POST.
7. **Library truth:** bare "false" in the phone list, "all caught up" on a downloading show, no air dates.
8. **Watch Together layout:** the room sheet covers the whole picture on phones and the controls on tablets.
9. **Owner phone tabs:** 8 icon-only scrolling tabs; one "Owner" hub tab instead (max 5 labelled tabs).
10. **Gold-bulb discipline:** the season page lights Keep all, every Play and every Keep link in accent gold; one
    lit thing per screen (verified at 390 px).

Design system: no big-bang rewrite. Commit a CSS drift budget in CI (counts may not rise), migrate tokens as files
are touched, one dedicated font-size batch later (floors = today's px so phones diff to zero).

## Features (final votes merged)
1. **Watch privately** — before the in-progress profiles/diary work goes live (it would otherwise show friends
   everyone's past viewing, family included). All three panelists top-3.
2. **Up next for the whole Watch Together room** (+ "pause please" for guests).
3. **Lock-screen / headphone controls + keep the phone awake while it's the TV remote** (Media Session, Wake Lock).
4. **Season tabs** on one shared episode list with air dates (chip row; "All seasons" sheet above 8, for One Piece).
5. **Search shows what's on Lilin first**, with can-I-watch-it-now marks on cards.
6. **Readable TV text + subtitle styles** — in the Rust TV app (the parents' Samsung runs it, not public/tv).
Next tier: after-credits chip (TMDB keywords), send a title to a friend + "From friends" shelf, collection strip,
skip recap via TheIntroDB (privacy + cut mismatch caveats), personal billboard with "ebi loved it" reasons.

Killed/deferred: vertical clip feed, ambient glow, poster→title morph (fights the sheet animation), AI search/recaps
(would run on magi), home-row editor, taste-match %, separate diary (profiles covers it), "Ask for it" (all 35
users can download), "Who's watching" on TV (until someone reports two people sharing one TV account).
